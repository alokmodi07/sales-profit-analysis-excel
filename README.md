\# Sales \& Profit Analysis Dashboard — Excel



\## 📊 Project Overview



This project analyzes sales and profitability data using Microsoft Excel to identify business performance trends, profitable categories, monthly patterns, and the impact of discounts on profit.



The analysis uses PivotTables, calculated fields, charts, and KPI summaries to transform raw order data into actionable business insights.



\## 🎯 Business Objectives



\- Analyze overall sales and profit performance

\- Identify profitable and less profitable product categories

\- Track monthly sales and profit trends

\- Analyze the relationship between discounts and profit

\- Compare category-level performance

\- Build an interactive Excel dashboard for business reporting

\## 🔍 Analysis Performed



\### 1. Sales \& Profit Analysis

Used PivotTables to analyze sales and profit across different business dimensions.



\### 2. Category Analysis

Compared categories based on sales and profit to identify areas contributing to overall business performance.



\### 3. Monthly Trend Analysis

Analyzed monthly sales and profit to identify changes in performance over time.



\### 4. Profit Margin Analysis

Created a calculated Profit Margin field to evaluate profitability relative to sales.



\### 5. Discount Analysis

Examined how discount levels relate to profitability and identified cases where higher discounts were associated with lower or negative profit.
